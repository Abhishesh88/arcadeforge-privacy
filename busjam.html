Privacy Policy — Bus Jam

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Bus Jam ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. We built the App to be fun and respectful of your privacy. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information that identifies you.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or any similar personal information. The App has no login and no user accounts.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress — such as your level, coins, unlocked items, best scores, and settings — locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information, which can include your device's advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location inferred from IP address, device information, and ad-interaction data. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us.

  • Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings under Google → Ads.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Data security

Because game data is stored locally on your device and we do not operate servers that hold your personal data, your game information remains under your control on your device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
